### Course Outcomes (CO) Mapping

- **CO1 (Unit I):** Design export strategies integrating the World Trade Organization (WTO) multilateral framework, Indian government and semi-government promotion agencies, statutory steps, and aligned documentation.
- **CO2 (Unit II):** Evaluate international trade operations, regulatory frameworks (FT(D&R) Act, Foreign Trade Policy, Customs Act, FEMA), export sales contracts (INCOTERMS 2020), payment mechanisms (UCP 600, URC 522), shipment procedures, central excise/GST treatment, and customs EDI automation.
- **CO3 (Unit III):** Develop risk-mitigation, trade finance, and operational clearance plans covering pre-shipment packing credit, international banking roles, export factoring and forfaiting, marine cargo insurance, quality inspection regimes (EIC/EIA), customs clearance workflows, and the Harmonized System (HS/ITC-HS) of classification.

### Mark-to-Length & Time Allocation Guide

In standard 3-hour (180 minutes) university examinations with 70–100 total marks:

| Question Weightage | Target Length | Recommended Time | Core Structure | Expected Components |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2 Marks** | 30–50 words (2–4 lines) | 2–3 minutes | Definition / Concept + 1 key statutory term | Precise formula, section, or official full form. No waffle. |
| **5 Marks** | 150–200 words (1 to 1.5 pages) | 7–9 minutes | Intro (1 para) + 4–5 bullet points + Indian Example | Structured headings, statutory authority, short table/box if distinguishing. |
| **10 Marks** | 400–500 words (2.5 to 3.5 pages) | 16–18 minutes | Intro + Detailed Analytical Body + Flowchart/Table + Case/Example + Conclusion | Legal act/rule, step-by-step procedure, administrative agencies, policy rationale. |
| **15 Marks** | 700–900 words (4.5 to 6 pages) | 24–28 minutes | Exhaustive structural treatment: Background + Complete Legal Framework + Diagram + In-depth Analysis + Risk/Operational Nuance + Conclusion | Critical evaluation, procedural flowchart, practical Indian exporter context (e.g., engineering goods or garment exports). |
