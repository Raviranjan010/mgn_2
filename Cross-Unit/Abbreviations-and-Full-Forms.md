# Cross-Unit Abbreviations & Full Forms: International Trade Master Dictionary

**Course:** MGN902: Export Import Procedure and Documentation  
**Scope:** Units I, II, and III  
**Pedagogical Note:** Organized alphabetically with exact official full forms and concise, one-line practical explanations of their role in foreign trade operations.

---

| Abbreviation | Official Statutory / Trade Full Form | Practical Role & Institutional Context |
|---|---|---|
| **AD** | Authorized Dealer (Category-I) | Commercial bank licensed by RBI under FEMA to handle foreign exchange remittances. |
| **ADS** | Aligned Documentation System | Standardized UNLK-based trade documentation layout system on A4 paper. |
| **AEO** | Authorized Economic Operator | Customs compliance certification granting Green Channel fast-track clearance. |
| **AEPC** | Apparel Export Promotion Council | Sectoral export promotion council for India's ready-made garments industry. |
| **Agmark** | Agricultural Marketing | Quality certification mark for Indian agricultural produce issued by DMI. |
| **AoA** | Agreement on Agriculture | WTO multilateral treaty regulating agricultural tariffs and subsidies (Green/Amber/Blue boxes). |
| **APEDA** | Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority | Statutory body under Ministry of Commerce promoting agro and processed food exports. |
| **ARE-1** | Application for Removal of Excisable Goods for Export | Traditional statutory excise form used to clear manufactured export cargo under rebate. |
| **AWB** | Air Waybill | Non-negotiable transport document and cargo receipt issued by air carriers; not document of title. |
| **B/E** | Bill of Entry | Legal import declaration filed on ICEGATE under Section 46 of Customs Act, 1962. |
| **B/L** | Bill of Lading (Ocean) | Negotiable document of title, receipt, and contract of carriage issued by ocean shipping lines. |
| **BAF** | Bunker Adjustment Factor | Ocean freight surcharge levied by shipping lines to compensate for marine fuel price volatility. |
| **BIS** | Bureau of Indian Standards | National standards body of India administering product quality standards and the ISI mark. |
| **BRC** | Bank Realization Certificate | Bank certificate confirming realization of foreign currency for an export consignment. |
| **CAF** | Currency Adjustment Factor | Ocean freight surcharge levied to offset bilateral currency exchange rate fluctuations. |
| **CBIC** | Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs | Apex Indian tax body under Ministry of Finance administering customs and GST. |
| **CFR** | Cost and Freight | Incoterms 2020 maritime rule where seller pays freight to destination; risk transfers at origin. |
| **CFS** | Container Freight Station | Customs-bonded port-adjacent facility where container cargo is stuffed, destuffed, and examined. |
| **CHA** | Custom House Agent (Customs Broker) | Licensed agent authorized to act on behalf of exporters/importers for customs clearance. |
| **CIF** | Cost, Insurance and Freight | Incoterms 2020 maritime rule; seller pays freight and minimum ICC (C) insurance to destination. |
| **CIP** | Carriage and Insurance Paid To | Incoterms 2020 multimodal rule; seller pays carriage and maximum ICC (A) insurance to destination. |
| **CISG** | UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods | Uniform international treaty governing formation and obligations in cross-border sales contracts. |
| **COO** | Certificate of Origin | Statutory document certifying the country where goods were grown, manufactured, or processed. |
| **CPT** | Carriage Paid To | Incoterms 2020 multimodal rule where seller pays freight to destination; risk transfers at origin. |
| **CT-1** | Central Excise Form CT-1 | Traditional procurement certificate allowing duty-free raw material sourcing for export production. |
| **CVD** | Countervailing Duty | Targeted customs tariff neutralizing foreign government subsidies granted to imported goods. |
| **D/A** | Documents Against Acceptance | Documentary collection under URC 522 where documents are released upon buyer accepting a time bill. |
| **D/P** | Documents Against Payment | Documentary collection under URC 522 where documents are released only upon full cash payment at sight. |
| **DAP** | Delivered at Place | Incoterms 2020 multimodal rule where seller delivers goods to foreign destination ready for unloading. |
| **DDP** | Delivered Duty Paid | Incoterms 2020 rule imposing maximum seller liability; delivered to door with import duties fully paid. |
| **DGCIS** | Directorate General of Commercial Intelligence and Statistics | Kolkata-based agency compiling and publishing India's national foreign trade statistics. |
| **DGFT** | Directorate General of Foreign Trade | Apex administrative body in New Delhi formulating FTP and issuing Importer-Exporter Codes (IEC). |
| **DMI** | Directorate of Marketing and Inspection | Ministry of Agriculture division administering statutory grading and Agmark quality standards. |
| **DPU** | Delivered at Place Unloaded | Incoterms 2020 rule (replaced DAT) where seller delivers and physically unloads cargo at destination. |
| **DSC** | Digital Signature Certificate | Cryptographic token required for signing declarations on ICEGATE and DGFT portals. |
| **DSU** | Dispute Settlement Understanding | WTO treaty providing the framework, panels, and timelines for resolving member trade disputes. |
| **e-BRC** | Electronic Bank Realization Certificate | Digital certificate transmitted by AD banks to DGFT confirming foreign exchange realization. |
| **ECL** | Electronic Cash Ledger | Online wallet on ICEGATE allowing instant advance settlement of customs import duties. |
| **ECGC** | Export Credit Guarantee Corporation of India | State-owned credit insurer protecting exporters against foreign buyer payment defaults. |
| **EDI** | Electronic Data Interchange | Computer-to-computer electronic transmission of structured business data without human intervention. |
| **EDIFACT** | Electronic Data Interchange for Administration, Commerce and Transport | United Nations international electronic syntax standard for customs and commercial messages. |
| **EDPMS** | Export Data Processing and Monitoring System | RBI real-time online platform monitoring and reconciling export shipping bills against bank remittances. |
| **EEFC** | Exchange Earners' Foreign Currency Account | Non-interest-bearing bank account allowing Indian exporters to retain 100% of foreign currency earnings. |
| **EEPC** | Engineering Export Promotion Council | Premier export promotion council for Indian engineering and manufacturing exports. |
| **EIA** | Export Inspection Agency | Field network of 5 regional statutory testing bodies operating under the EIC at Indian ports. |
| **EIC** | Export Inspection Council | Apex statutory body established under Export (QC&I) Act, 1963, to regulate export quality. |
| **EPC** | Export Promotion Council | Non-profit sector-specific trade promotion association issuing statutory RCMCs to exporters. |
| **EPCG** | Export Promotion Capital Goods | Trade scheme allowing duty-free machinery imports against fulfillment of a 6x export obligation. |
| **EXW** | Ex Works | Incoterms 2020 rule imposing minimum seller liability; delivery occurs at seller's factory/warehouse. |
| **FAS** | Free Alongside Ship | Incoterms 2020 maritime rule where delivery occurs when goods are placed alongside vessel at port. |
| **FCA** | Free Carrier | Incoterms 2020 multimodal rule where seller delivers goods to buyer's carrier at named place. |
| **FCI** | Factors Chain International | Global association of commercial factoring companies regulating two-factor international factoring. |
| **FCL** | Full Container Load | Dedicated single-shipper shipping container loaded at factory or CFS under exclusive customs seal. |
| **FEMA** | Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 | Apex Indian statute regulating foreign exchange transactions, external payments, and realization. |
| **FIEO** | Federation of Indian Export Organisations | Apex coordinating body for all Indian export promotion councils and multi-product export houses. |
| **FIRC** | Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate | Physical or digital certificate issued by AD banks confirming inward cross-border remittances. |
| **FOB** | Free On Board | Incoterms 2020 maritime rule where risk and cost transfer when goods pass on board the ocean vessel. |
| **FTA** | Free Trade Agreement | Preferential bilateral/plurilateral treaty eliminating customs duties on traded goods between members. |
| **FTDR** | Foreign Trade (Development & Regulation) Act, 1992 | Apex statute empowering Indian government to formulate Foreign Trade Policy and regulate trade. |
| **FTP** | Foreign Trade Policy | National economic policy governing export incentives, regulatory frameworks, and import rules. |
| **GATS** | General Agreement on Trade in Services | Multilateral WTO agreement regulating cross-border trade in commercial services across 4 modes. |
| **GATT** | General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade | Multilateral trade agreement (1947) focused on reducing border tariffs; predecessor to the WTO. |
| **GRI** | General Rules of Interpretation | Six universal legal interpretation rules (Rules 1 to 6) governing classification under the HS code. |
| **GRIF** | General Rules for International Factoring | Standard contractual rules promulgated by FCI governing cross-border two-factor factoring. |
| **GSP** | Generalized System of Preferences | Non-reciprocal preferential tariff system granted by developed nations to developing country exports. |
| **GST** | Goods and Services Tax | National comprehensive indirect tax subsuming central excise, VAT, and service tax since 2017. |
| **HACCP** | Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point | Preventive food safety management system mandatory for processed food and marine exports. |
| **HBP** | Handbook of Procedures | Operational manual published by DGFT establishing day-to-day administrative EXIM procedures. |
| **HS** | Harmonized System | Universal 6-digit commodity classification nomenclature administered by the WCO (Brussels). |
| **ICC** | International Chamber of Commerce | Apex global private business organization in Paris that publishes Incoterms 2020 and UCP 600. |
| **ICC (A/B/C)**| Institute Cargo Clauses (A, B, C) | Standardized marine insurance clauses defining the scope of covered transit casualties and risks. |
| **ICD** | Inland Container Depot | Dry port facility equipped with customs inspection and cargo handling located in the hinterland. |
| **ICEGATE**| Indian Customs Electronic Gateway | National portal (`icegate.gov.in`) connecting trade entities to customs for electronic filing. |
| **ICES** | Indian Customs EDI System | Automated software engine used internally by Indian customs officers for document appraisal. |
| **IDPMS** | Import Data Processing and Monitoring System | RBI automated portal monitoring import payments against physical arrival of goods in India. |
| **IEC** | Importer-Exporter Code | 10-digit PAN-based registration number issued by DGFT mandatory for conducting cross-border trade. |
| **IES** | Interest Equalisation Scheme | Central government export incentive providing a 2% to 3% interest subvention on pre/post-shipment loans. |
| **IGM** | Import General Manifest | Legal manifest filed by inbound carrier under Section 30 of Customs Act prior to vessel arrival. |
| **IGST** | Integrated Goods and Services Tax | Tax levied on interstate supplies and imports; exports are zero-rated under Section 16 of IGST Act. |
| **IIFT** | Indian Institute of Foreign Trade | Autonomous academic and trade research institution in New Delhi under Ministry of Commerce. |
| **IIP** | Indian Institute of Packaging | Packaging research and testing body in Mumbai administering mandatory UN certifications. |
| **IPQC** | In-Process Quality Control | Pre-shipment quality inspection methodology auditing manufacturing processes at plant level. |
| **ISPM-15**| International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15 | Global standard governing heat or chemical (methyl bromide) fumigation of wood packaging material. |
| **ITC(HS)** | Indian Trade Clarification based on Harmonized System | India's national 8-digit commodity classification code used for customs duty and import licensing. |
| **ITPO** | India Trade Promotion Organisation | Premier trade exhibition body managing Pragati Maidan and overseas Indian industrial fairs. |
| **L/C** | Letter of Credit | Irrevocable bank payment undertaking guaranteeing payment against strictly compliant documents. |
| **LCL** | Less than Container Load | Consolidated ocean container shipment combining cargo lots from multiple different exporters. |
| **LEO** | Let Export Order | Statutory customs clearance order issued under Section 51 of Customs Act permitting vessel loading. |
| **LoC** | Line of Credit | Concessional sovereign loan extended by EXIM Bank of India to foreign governments for Indian goods. |
| **LUT** | Letter of Undertaking | GST bond executed online enabling exporters to export goods/services without paying upfront IGST. |
| **MEIS** | Merchandise Exports from India Scheme | Historical scrip-based FTP export incentive (subsumed by RoDTEP due to WTO dispute rulings). |
| **MFN** | Most-Favoured-Nation | GATT Article I principle mandating non-discriminatory equal treatment to all WTO trading partners. |
| **MMTC** | Metals and Minerals Trading Corporation | State trading enterprise handling bulk sovereign imports of gold bullion and canalised fertilizer exports. |
| **MPEDA** | Marine Products Export Development Authority | Statutory body under Ministry of Commerce promoting seafood exports and processing standards. |
| **MSME** | Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises | Commercial enterprises entitled to priority lending and higher interest subvention benefits (IES). |
| **NVOCC** | Non-Vessel Operating Common Carrier | Ocean freight consolidator issuing House Bills of Lading without owning or operating ocean vessels. |
| **OOC** | Out of Charge | Statutory customs clearance order issued under Section 47 of Customs Act permitting release of imports. |
| **PCFC** | Packing Credit in Foreign Currency | Concessional pre-shipment bank loan in foreign currency linked to SOFR/EURIBOR, providing natural hedge. |
| **PGA** | Partner Government Agencies | Regulatory agencies (FSSAI, Plant/Animal Quarantine, Drug Controller) integrated into SWIFT. |
| **POD** | Port of Discharge | Maritime port where cargo is physically discharged from the carrying ocean vessel. |
| **POL** | Port of Loading | Maritime port where cargo is hoisted and secured on board the ocean vessel. |
| **QC&I** | Quality Control and Inspection | Regulatory regime enforced under the Export (Quality Control and Inspection) Act, 1963. |
| **QR** | Quantitative Restrictions | Numerical limits or quotas imposed on imported or exported goods (banned under GATT Art. XI). |
| **RCMC** | Registration-cum-Membership Certificate | Statutory certificate issued by EPCs/Commodity Boards mandatory for claiming FTP export incentives. |
| **RMS** | Risk Management System | Automated customs module facilitating low-risk cargo through the Green Channel without inspection. |
| **RoDTEP**| Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products | Scheme refunding unrebated embedded central, state, and local taxes on exported goods. |
| **RoSCTL**| Remission of State and Central Taxes and Levies | Specialized remission scheme for Indian export apparel and made-up textile products. |
| **SBLC** | Standby Letter of Credit | Financial backstop bank guarantee payable only upon default in applicant's underlying obligation. |
| **SCM** | Subsidies and Countervailing Measures | WTO agreement regulating trade-distorting government subsidies and permitting countervailing duties. |
| **SCOMET** | Special Chemicals, Organisms, Materials, Equipment & Technologies | Dual-use export control list subject to stringent inter-ministerial licensing in India. |
| **SEZ** | Special Economic Zone | Specifically delineated duty-free trade enclave treated as foreign territory for tariff purposes. |
| **SIAC** | Singapore International Arbitration Centre | Leading institutional arbitral body frequently designated in Asian cross-border trade contracts. |
| **SOFR** | Secured Overnight Financing Rate | Broad benchmark interest rate for dollar-denominated loans, replacing historical USD LIBOR in PCFC. |
| **SPS** | Sanitary and Phytosanitary Measures | WTO agreement governing food safety and animal/plant quarantine standards. |
| **STC** | State Trading Corporation of India | Premier canalised state enterprise handling essential sovereign imports (pulses, edible oils). |
| **STE** | State Trading Enterprise | Government-sanctioned exclusive canalised enterprise handling designated state commodities. |
| **SWIFT** | Single Window Interface for Facilitating Trade | Indian customs system on ICEGATE integrating customs with 6 Partner Government Agencies. |
| **TBT** | Technical Barriers to Trade | WTO agreement ensuring mandatory product standards and testing do not create non-tariff barriers. |
| **TFA** | Trade Facilitation Agreement | Landmark WTO treaty expediting cross-border customs clearance and transparency. |
| **TPCI** | Trade Promotion Council of India | Apex trade organization promoting food, beverage, and consumer goods exports (IndusFood). |
| **TRIMS** | Trade-Related Investment Measures | WTO agreement banning investment conditions like local content requirements (LCRs). |
| **TRIPS** | Trade-Related Aspects of Intellectual Property Rights | WTO agreement establishing minimum global legal standards for patents, copyrights, and trademarks. |
| **UCP 600**| Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits | Standardized ICC rules (Publication 600) regulating international Letters of Credit. |
| **UN/EDIFACT** | UN Electronic Data Interchange for Administration, Commerce & Transport | Universal UN syntax standard governing international electronic customs messages. |
| **UNLK** | United Nations Layout Key | International standard (ISO 6422) establishing standard A4 dimensions for trade documents. |
| **URC 522**| Uniform Rules for Collections | Standardized ICC rules (Publication 522) regulating bank documentary collections (D/P and D/A). |
| **VAT** | Value Added Tax | State-level consumption tax largely subsumed by the Goods and Services Tax (GST) in 2017. |
| **WCO** | World Customs Organization | Brussels-based intergovernmental organization administering the global Harmonized System nomenclature. |
| **WMD** | Weapons of Mass Destruction | Proliferation of non-conventional arms controlled in trade through SCOMET regulations. |
| **WTO** | World Trade Organization | Geneva-based apex multilateral organization governing rules of trade between 164 member nations. |
