## 1. Golden Rules for 24-Hour Success

1. **Focus on Mechanics Before History:** Units II and III carry high-scoring procedural and numerical questions (Incoterms, Payment Methods/LCs, Customs Clearance, Pre-Shipment Finance, Insurance). Master these first.
2. **Prioritize Visuals and Tables:** In Indian university exams, evaluators scan for neat tables and flowcharts. Drawing a 6-step LC flow or an 11-rule Incoterms matrix earns 80% of marks before the evaluator even reads your text.
3. **Respect the Tags:**
   - **[MUST]:** Core concepts tested in 85%+ of exam papers. Non-negotiable.
   - **[SHOULD]:** High-probability 5-mark and 10-mark questions.
   - **[IF-TIME]:** Background history, peripheral agencies, and ultra-specific article subsections.

### Block 1: Unit II — International Trade Operations (08:00 – 12:30 | 4.5 Hours)
*Why start here? Unit II forms the mechanical backbone of international trade. Almost 40% of subjective marks come from Incoterms and Payment Methods.*

- **08:00 – 09:15 | Incoterms 2020 [MUST]**
  - Read `Unit-2-International-Trade-Operations/04-Incoterms.md` Section 1 ("In one minute") and Section 3.
  - Memorize the 4 groups: **E** (EXW), **F** (FCA, FAS, FOB), **C** (CPT, CIP, CFR, CIF), **D** (DAP, DPU, DDP).
  - Understand the golden rule: In C-terms (CIF, CFR, CIP, CPT), **risk transfers at the port of origin**, but the seller pays freight to destination.
  - Draw the Incoterms transfer matrix from `Cross-Unit/Diagrams.md`.
- **09:15 – 10:30 | Payment Methods & Letters of Credit (LC) [MUST]**
  - Read `Unit-2-International-Trade-Operations/03-Payment-Methods.md`.
  - Master the Payment Risk Spectrum: Advance Payment -> Documentary Credit (LC) -> Documentary Collection (D/P, D/A) -> Open Account.
  - Trace the 10-step LC transaction cycle: Applicant -> Issuing Bank -> Advising/Confirming Bank -> Beneficiary.
  - Memorize UCP 600 key rules: Article 14 (5 banking days standard for examination of documents), Article 4 (autonomy of credit), Article 34 (disclaimer on goods/documents).
- **10:30 – 11:30 | Shipment of Cargo & Customs Clearance [MUST]**
  - Read `Unit-2-International-Trade-Operations/05-Shipment-and-Customs.md`.
  - Memorize export milestones: Factory Stuffing -> CFS/ICD -> Shipping Bill filing -> Customs Examination -> Let Export Order (LEO) -> Mate's Receipt -> Bill of Lading.
  - Memorize import milestones: Import General Manifest (IGM) -> Bill of Entry (Sections 46, 47, 48) -> Assessment -> Duty Payment -> Out of Charge (OOC).
- **11:30 – 12:30 | Regulatory Framework, Sales Contract, EDI & Central Excise [SHOULD]**
  - Read `Unit-2-International-Trade-Operations/01-Regulatory-Framework.md` (FTDR Act 1992, FTP 2023 4 pillars, IEC).
  - Read `Unit-2-International-Trade-Operations/02-Sales-Contract.md` (Offer, Acceptance, Essential Clauses, Force Majeure).
  - Read `Unit-2-International-Trade-Operations/07-EDI.md` (ICES, ICEGATE, Risk Management System - RMS, SWIFT).
  - Read `Unit-2-International-Trade-Operations/06-Central-Excise.md` (Traditional rebate/bond vs GST LUT mechanism).

### Block 2: Unit III — Export Finance, Risk & Quality (13:30 – 17:30 | 4.0 Hours)

- **13:30 – 14:30 | Pre-Shipment Finance (Packing Credit) [MUST]**
  - Read `Unit-3-Export-Finance-and-Risk/01-Pre-Shipment-Finance.md`.
  - Eligibility: Confirmed export order or Irrevocable LC.
  - Mechanics: Concessional interest rate, disbursement in stages, liquidation strictly through export proceeds (bills purchased/discounted).
  - PCFC (Packing Credit in Foreign Currency) benefits (hedges exchange risk at SOFR/EURIBOR linked rates).
- **14:30 – 15:30 | Marine Cargo Insurance & ECGC [MUST]**
  - Read `Unit-3-Export-Finance-and-Risk/04-Insurance.md`.
  - Institute Cargo Clauses: **ICC (A)** = All risks (widest); **ICC (B)** = Specified named perils (earthquake, washing overboard); **ICC (C)** = Major maritime casualties (narrowest).
  - General Average (voluntary sacrifice for common safety) vs Particular Average (accidental partial loss).
  - ECGC Standard Policy (Shipments Comprehensive Risks): Covers commercial risks (default, insolvency) up to 90%, and political risks (war, transfer delay) up to 90%. Does NOT cover buyer's rejection of sound goods.
- **15:30 – 16:30 | Factoring vs Forfaiting & Role of Banks [MUST]**
  - Read `Unit-3-Export-Finance-and-Risk/03-Factoring.md` and `02-Role-of-Banks.md`.
  - Two-Factor System: Export Factor + Import Factor (FCI framework).
  - Recourse vs Non-Recourse factoring.
  - Factoring vs Forfaiting distinction table (short-term vs medium/long-term capital goods, 80% advance vs 100% discount, promissory notes with aval).
  - EDPMS and IDPMS automated tracking by RBI.
- **16:30 – 17:30 | Harmonized System (HS) & Quality Inspection [SHOULD]**
  - Read `Unit-3-Export-Finance-and-Risk/08-Harmonized-System.md`.
  - HS Structure: 2 digits (Chapter), 4 digits (Heading), 6 digits (Subheading) -> 8 digits in Indian ITC(HS) (Tariff Item).
  - GRI Rules 1 to 6 (Rule 1: Terms of Headings; Rule 3(a): Specific over General; Rule 3(b): Essential Character).
  - Read `Unit-3-Export-Finance-and-Risk/06-Quality-and-Inspection.md` (Export QC&I Act 1963, EIA/EIC, Agmark, BIS, ISO).

### Block 3: Unit I — Institutional Framework & Documentation (18:30 – 21:30 | 3.0 Hours)

- **18:30 – 19:45 | WTO Multilateral Framework & Dispute Settlement [MUST]**
  - Read `Unit-1-Institutional-Framework/01-WTO.md`.
  - GATT (1947) vs WTO (1995) Uruguay Round transformation.
  - Pillars: Most-Favoured-Nation (MFN - Article I), National Treatment (Article III), Bound Tariffs.
  - DSU Mechanism: Consultations (60 days) -> Panel (6-9 months) -> Appellate Body -> Dispute Settlement Body adoption via **Negative/Reverse Consensus**.
  - Key Agreements: TRIPS, TRIMS, AoA (Green, Amber, Blue Boxes), SPS & TBT, Trade Facilitation Agreement (TFA).
- **19:45 – 20:30 | Steps for Successful Exporting [MUST]**
  - Read `Unit-1-Institutional-Framework/03-Steps-for-Exporting.md`.
  - 15 Steps: Setting up entity -> IEC -> RCMC from EPC -> AD Code -> Marketing -> Proforma Invoice -> Contract -> Pre-shipment finance -> Production -> Inspection -> Insurance -> Customs clearance (Shipping Bill) -> Port loading (B/L) -> Negotiation of documents -> Realization (e-BRC / EDPMS).
- **20:30 – 21:30 | Export Documentation & Agencies [SHOULD]**
  - Read `Unit-1-Institutional-Framework/04-Documentation.md`.
  - Aligned Documentation System (ADS) based on UN Layout Key (UNLK).
  - Commercial Documents (Invoice, Packing List, B/L, Insurance Certificate, Certificate of Origin).
  - Regulatory Documents (Shipping Bill, Bill of Entry, GR/SDF declaration).
  - Read `Unit-1-Institutional-Framework/02-Agencies.md` (DGFT, EPCs, FIEO, Commodity Boards, EXIM Bank).
