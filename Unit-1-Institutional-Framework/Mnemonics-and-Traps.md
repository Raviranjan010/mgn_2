### 1. High-Yield Memory Aids & Mnemonics

#### A. WTO Core Legal Principles: The "M-N-T" Rule
- **M** — **Most-Favoured-Nation (MFN - Art. I):** Treat all member countries equally. No discrimination at the border.
- **N** — **National Treatment (NT - Art. III):** Treat imported goods equal to domestic goods once inside your border.
- **T** — **Tariff Binding & Transparency (Art. II & X):** Commit to ceiling rates; publish all trade laws openly.
*Memory Hook:* Think of WTO as a **M**i**N**is**T**ry of fair global trade rules.

#### B. Major WTO Agreements: "G-G-T-T-A-S-T" ("Good Global Trade Takes A Smart Trader")
- **G** — **GATT** (General Agreement on Tariffs & Trade - Goods)
- **G** — **GATS** (General Agreement on Trade in Services)
- **T** — **TRIPS** (Trade-Related Aspects of Intellectual Property Rights)
- **T** — **TRIMS** (Trade-Related Investment Measures)
- **A** — **AoA** (Agreement on Agriculture: Green, Blue, Amber Boxes)
- **S** — **SPS & TBT** (Sanitary & Phytosanitary Measures / Technical Barriers to Trade)
- **T** — **TFA** (Trade Facilitation Agreement - Bali Package)

#### C. Export Promotion Agencies Ecosystem: "D-F-E-A-M-E-E-I-I-T" ("Dear Friends Export All Made-in-India Engineering Items Today")
- **D** — **DGFT** (Directorate General of Foreign Trade - Policy & IEC)
- **F** — **FIEO** (Federation of Indian Export Organisations - Apex Multi-Product Body)
- **E** — **EPCs** (Export Promotion Councils - Sectoral RCMC)
- **A** — **APEDA** (Agricultural & Processed Food Products Export Development Authority)
- **M** — **MPEDA** (Marine Products Export Development Authority)
- **E** — **EXIM Bank** (Lines of Credit & International Project Finance)
- **E** — **ECGC** (Export Credit Guarantee Corporation - Credit Insurance)
- **I** — **EIC / EIA** (Export Inspection Council / Agencies - Pre-shipment Quality)
- **I** — **IIP** (Indian Institute of Packaging - Packaging Standards & UN Testing)
- **T** — **ITPO** (India Trade Promotion Organisation - Pragati Maidan Trade Fairs)

#### D. The 15 Chronological Steps: "E-B-I-R-M | P-S-F-P-I | I-C-L-D-R"
Grouped into 3 logical phases:
- *Phase 1: Setup & Marketing (E-B-I-R-M)*
  1. **E**ntity registration (PAN)
  2. **B**ank account & AD Code registration
  3. **I**EC application online
  4. **R**CMC from relevant EPC
  5. **M**arket selection & buyer inquiry
- *Phase 2: Order to Production (P-S-F-P-I)*
  6. **P**roforma Invoice quotation
  7. **S**ales contract finalization
  8. **F**inance (Pre-shipment Packing Credit)
  9. **P**roduction & packaging (ISPM-15)
  10. **I**nspection (EIC/EIA Certificate)
- *Phase 3: Logistics & Realization (I-C-L-D-R)*
  11. **I**nsurance policy (Marine ICC)
  12. **C**ustoms clearance (Shipping Bill & LEO)
  13. **L**oading onto ship & Bill of Lading (B/L)
  14. **D**ocumentary negotiation with AD Bank
  15. **R**ealization of foreign exchange (e-BRC / EDPMS within 9 months)

### 2. Top 20 Examiner Traps & Misconceptions

| # | What Students Often Think (The Trap) | What is Actually True (The Reality) | Why Examiners Test It |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MFN means granting special, preferential, or superior treatment to your favourite trading partner. | MFN means **equal treatment to all WTO members**. You cannot give special favours to one country without extending them to everyone. | Tests understanding of non-discrimination (GATT Art. I). |
| 2 | National Treatment applies before goods cross customs at the port. | National Treatment applies **strictly AFTER goods have entered the domestic market** and paid customs duty. Border tariffs are permitted under WTO; discriminatory domestic taxes are not. | Tests distinction between GATT Art. I/II and Art. III. |
| 3 | In the WTO Dispute Settlement Body, reports are adopted by simple majority vote. | Reports are adopted by **Reverse / Negative Consensus** — meaning a report is adopted automatically unless *every single member* (including the winner) votes against it. | Foundational procedural rule of DSU. |
| 4 | An Air Waybill (AWB) is a document of title just like an Ocean Bill of Lading. | An Air Waybill is **NEVER a document of title**. It is only a receipt for goods and an evidence of contract of carriage. Goods by air are released to the named consignee upon identification, without presenting the original AWB. | Classic 2-mark and distinction trap. |
| 5 | An IEC (Importer-Exporter Code) has to be renewed every year by paying a fee. | IEC is valid for life. However, under FTP provisions, an exporter must **electronically update/confirm IEC details annually** (between April and June) on the DGFT portal; failure deactivates it without cancelling. | Practical operational knowledge. |
| 6 | DGFT conducts physical customs checks and collects customs duty at sea ports. | DGFT is an administrative/policy body under the Ministry of Commerce. Customs duties are collected by **CBIC (Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs)** under the Ministry of Finance. | Cross-ministry boundary confusion. |
| 7 | FIEO is an Export Promotion Council for engineering products. | FIEO is the **apex coordinating body** for all export promotion organizations; EEPC handles engineering products. | Entity mandate confusion. |
| 8 | ECGC provides loans and advances money directly to exporters. | ECGC is an **insurance company**, not a bank. It provides credit insurance policies to exporters and credit guarantees to commercial banks. EXIM Bank and commercial banks provide loans. | Bank vs Insurer confusion. |
| 9 | Green Box subsidies under the WTO Agreement on Agriculture are subject to strict monetary limits. | Green Box subsidies (government R&D, pest management, disaster relief) have **no monetary ceiling** because they do not distort trade. Amber Box subsidies have limits (De Minimis). | WTO AoA classification trap. |
| 10 | The WTO Appellate Body is currently issuing final binding judgments on all disputes. | The Appellate Body has been **functionally paralyzed since December 2019** because the United States blocked appointment of new judges, leaving it without the statutory 3-member quorum. | Current affairs legal nuance. |
| 11 | Export proceeds can be realized in India within 2 to 3 years if the buyer requests it. | Under FEMA 1999 regulations, export proceeds must be realized and repatriated within **9 months** from the date of shipment (15 months for warehousing abroad). | Core statutory deadline. |
| 12 | A Clean Bill of Lading means the ship or container has been physically washed and cleaned. | A Clean B/L means the carrier noted **no defects in the external condition or packaging** of the cargo upon loading. A Claused/Foul B/L contains remarks noting damaged packaging. | Standard UCP 600 terminology trap. |
| 13 | The Packing List shows the unit price and total monetary value of the goods. | A Packing List **omits monetary values and prices**. It focuses exclusively on physical quantities, packaging dimensions, net weight, gross weight, container number, and box marks. | Document function separation. |
| 14 | Certificate of Origin is only needed if goods are toxic or hazardous. | A Certificate of Origin is needed for **almost every commercial shipment** to determine import duty rates (especially preferential tariff concessions under FTAs) and verify trade embargoes. | Commercial reality check. |
| 15 | Phytosanitary certificates are issued by the Marine Products Export Development Authority. | Phytosanitary certificates are issued by the **Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage** (for plant/agro products). MPEDA does not issue phytosanitary certificates. | Institutional specialization. |
| 16 | Aligned Documentation System (ADS) requires 16 completely separate manual handwriting entries on different paper sizes. | ADS uses standardized **A4 paper based on the UN Layout Key (UNLK)**, enabling all commercial documents to be printed from a single master document layout without re-keying data. | Efficiency purpose of ADS. |
| 17 | STC (State Trading Corporation) and MMTC handle private courier parcels. | STC and MMTC are **Canalised State Trading Enterprises** handling bulk sovereign commodities (minerals, fertilizer, gold bullion, food security grains). | Agency scale confusion. |
| 18 | Mate's Receipt is issued by the customs appraiser at the port gate. | Mate's Receipt is issued by the **Chief Officer / Mate of the vessel** after the cargo is physically loaded on board. | Port operational sequence. |
| 19 | TRIMS agreement permits developing countries to force foreign investors to buy 50% local parts. | TRIMS **strictly prohibits Local Content Requirements (LCRs)** and foreign exchange balancing conditions as violations of GATT Article III (National Treatment). | TRIMS compliance trap. |
| 20 | Indian exporters claim duty drawback directly from the WTO office in Geneva. | Duty Drawback is claimed from the **Indian Customs Department (CBIC)** under Sections 74 and 75 of the Customs Act, 1962. | Institutional hierarchy trap. |

### 3. Top 50 Rapid-Fire Facts for Instant Recall

1. **WTO Establishment Date:** 1 January 1995.
2. **Treaty that created WTO:** Marrakesh Agreement (April 1994).
3. **Predecessor to WTO:** General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT 1947).
4. **Number of WTO Member States:** 164 member nations.
5. **Highest Decision-Making Body in WTO:** Ministerial Conference (meets at least once every 2 years).
6. **Day-to-day WTO governing organ:** General Council (based in Geneva).
7. **WTO Dispute Settlement mandatory consultation period:** 60 days.
8. **Standard time for WTO Dispute Panel to issue report:** 6 to 9 months.
9. **Reverse Consensus rule:** A DSB decision is adopted unless *all* members vote against it.
10. **GATT Article I:** Most-Favoured-Nation (MFN) principle.
11. **GATT Article III:** National Treatment principle.
12. **GATT Article VI:** Anti-Dumping and Countervailing Duties.
13. **GATT Article XI:** General Elimination of Quantitative Restrictions (quotas).
14. **GATS Mode 1:** Cross-Border Supply (e.g., Indian BPO call centre serving US client).
15. **GATS Mode 2:** Consumption Abroad (e.g., foreign medical tourist receiving care in India).
16. **GATS Mode 3:** Commercial Presence (e.g., Indian IT company opening a branch in London).
17. **GATS Mode 4:** Movement of Natural Persons (e.g., Indian software consultant travelling to Munich).
18. **TRIPS Patent protection duration:** Minimum 20 years from filing date.
19. **AoA De Minimis limit for India (developing):** 10% of total agricultural production value.
20. **AoA Green Box Subsidies:** Decoupled from production; non-trade-distorting; zero ceiling.
21. **TFA (Trade Facilitation Agreement):** Concluded at Bali Ministerial (2013); in force 2017.
22. **FT(D&R) Act Year:** 1992 (Foreign Trade Development & Regulation Act).
23. **Head of DGFT:** Directorate General of Foreign Trade (Headquarters: New Delhi).
24. **Number of digits in IEC:** 10 digits (alphanumeric, identical to PAN).
25. **Portal for IEC application:** `dgft.gov.in`.
26. **RCMC Full Form:** Registration-cum-Membership Certificate.
27. **Validity period of RCMC:** Typically 5 years (valid from 1 April of year of issue).
28. **FIEO Headquarters:** New Delhi.
29. **DGCIS Location:** Kolkata, West Bengal.
30. **EXIM Bank Establishment Year:** 1982 (Act of 1981).
31. **ECGC Ownership:** 100% Government of India enterprise.
32. **EIC Headquarters:** New Delhi (established under Export QC&I Act, 1963).
33. **Number of Regional Export Inspection Agencies (EIAs):** 5 (Mumbai, Kolkata, Kochi, Delhi, Chennai).
34. **Indian Institute of Packaging (IIP) Location:** Mumbai (main campus).
35. **Statutory realization period for export proceeds:** 9 months from shipment date (under FEMA).
36. **AD Code Length:** 14-digit numeric code assigned by RBI-authorized dealer bank.
37. **e-BRC Full Form:** Electronic Bank Realization Certificate.
38. **EDPMS Full Form:** Export Data Processing and Monitoring System (RBI).
39. **ADS Basis:** United Nations Layout Key (UNLK) for trade documents (ISO 6422).
40. **Total number of documents in full ADS aligned series:** 20 documents (16 Commercial + 4 Regulatory).
41. **Number of Principal Commercial Documents in ADS:** 8 documents.
42. **Number of Auxiliary Commercial Documents in ADS:** 8 documents.
43. **Three legal attributes of Ocean Bill of Lading:** (1) Cargo Receipt, (2) Document of Title, (3) Contract of Carriage.
44. **Does Air Waybill confer title to goods?** NO.
45. **Number of original Bills of Lading typically issued in a negotiable set:** 3 originals (3/3).
46. **Mate's Receipt issuer:** Chief Officer (First Mate) of the ocean vessel.
47. **Let Export Order (LEO) section:** Section 51 of Customs Act, 1962.
48. **Wood packaging fumigation standard:** ISPM-15 (International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures).
49. **Digital platform for Indian Preferential Certificate of Origin:** `coo.dgft.gov.in`.
50. **Zero-Rated Supply statute for Indian exports:** Section 16 of the IGST Act, 2017.
